The short version

Traffic Escape has no accounts and no server of its own. Your data sits in two places: on your phone, and, as anonymous usage data, in our Google Firebase project. Each can be deleted as below.

1. Stop sending usage data

  1. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland: open Traffic Escape, go to Settings → Privacy & Terms and switch Share usage data off. Nothing more is sent from then on.
  2. Everywhere else the game has no switch for this: uninstall the game to stop sending usage data.

2. Delete your progress on your phone

  1. Open Android Settings → Apps → Traffic Escape → Storage.
  2. Tap Clear storage, or uninstall the game. Your progress, stars, hints, streak and settings are erased at once. The game is not backed up, so no other copy exists.

3. Ask us to delete usage data

Email goldengarudastudios@gmail.com with the subject Traffic Escape data deletion. We reply within 30 days and delete whatever we can link to your request.

This data carries no name or email address, only a random identifier for one installation of the game, so we may not be able to pick out your records from an email alone. Either way it does not outlive the retention period below.

What is deleted, what is kept, and for how long

  • Progress, stars, hints, streak and settings (on your phone): deleted immediately in step 2. We never hold a copy.
  • Usage data (Firebase Analytics): kept for at most 14 months, then deleted automatically.
  • Your email to us: kept only as long as needed to answer it.

Nothing else is kept. Ratings and reviews you publish on Google Play are managed in the Play Store, not by us. See the Privacy Policy for what each kind of data contains.
