Traffic Escape

Clear the jam. One car at a time.

Traffic Escape is a traffic puzzle game for Android. It shows no ads, sells nothing and has no accounts. Anonymous usage data goes to Google's Firebase (in Europe only if you say yes), and you can switch it off or have it deleted at any time.

  • Privacy Policy What the game stores on your device, what it sends, and what it never does.
  • Terms of Service The terms you agree to by installing or playing the game.
  • Delete your data How to stop sharing usage data and delete what the game and Firebase hold.
