Traffic Escape

Privacy Policy

Google Play package oreo.traffic.escape.cars.puzzle
Published by Golden Garuda Studios

Last updated 3 November 2026.

The short version

Traffic Escape keeps your progress and settings on your device. It shows no ads, sells nothing and has no accounts. Only one thing can leave your device: anonymous usage data, sent to Google's Firebase so we can see how the levels play and improve them. In Europe it is sent only if you say yes, and anyone can switch it off in the game's Settings → Privacy & Terms.

What the game stores on your device

One private save file holding only what the game needs: the levels you have cleared with their stars and best move counts, your hints, the daily puzzles you have solved and your streak, the car style, colours and board you picked, your settings (sound, music, vibration, reduced motion, and whether usage data is shared) and when the game last asked you for a rating. A second private file keeps a short log of the same game events listed below, for checking the game works; it never leaves your device and is cleared once it grows past 1 MB. Android removes both files when you uninstall the game, clearing the app's data in Android Settings erases them at once, and they are not copied to any backup or server.

Sharing usage data

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, and on any device whose region the game cannot tell, nothing is shared until you switch on Share usage data in the game's Settings → Privacy & Terms. Everywhere else it starts on, since the data is anonymous and used only to improve the game. Wherever you are, switching it off stops all further sharing straight away. The game works out your region from your device's region setting and makes no network call to locate you.

Firebase Analytics

While sharing is on, Firebase Analytics receives game events: a level started, finished (with moves, par, stars, time taken, and hints, undos and restarts used), restarted or left; a hint used or earned; a setting switched; a car style, colour or board chosen; your answer when the game asks for a rating. Firebase adds standard technical details: the app version, your device model and Android version, your approximate country (derived from your network address, which is not stored), and a random identifier for this installation of the game. We use this only to see how the game is played and to improve it.

Nothing personal, nothing for ads

None of it contains your name, email address, contacts, photos or location. The game tells Firebase that consent for advertising storage, advertising user data and ad personalization is denied, so nothing collected is used for advertising. Firebase can read Android's advertising identifier on devices where it is available; with advertising consent denied it is not used for ads, and you can delete or reset it in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.

How long it is kept

Usage data is processed by Google under the Firebase privacy terms and kept for at most 14 months, then deleted automatically. To have data already collected from your device deleted, see Delete your data, or email us.

Google Play

The game asks Google Play whether a newer version has been available for at least a day and, if so, shows Google Play's update screen. After you have cleared some levels it may ask whether you enjoy the game; if you say yes, Google Play's own rating card is shown on the home screen, and Rate Traffic Escape in Settings opens the game's Play Store page. These are handled by Google Play under Google's own terms. We see the ratings and reviews you choose to publish on the Play Store, as anyone can, and nothing else from them.

Permissions

VIBRATE for the small haptic taps, which you can switch off in Settings. INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, added by the Firebase and Google Play libraries, for usage data, updates and ratings. AD_ID is added by Firebase; see above. The game shows no ads.

If you email us

We will see your email address and whatever you wrote, and we use it only to answer you, keeping it only as long as needed to do so.

What we do not do

We do not sell or rent any information about you. We do not show advertising, and nothing collected is used for advertising. We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location through the game.

Children

Traffic Escape is a puzzle game for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Usage data contains no names or contact details. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, email us and we will delete it.

Changes

If a future version changes what it collects or sends, for example by adding ads or purchases, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the change will be described in the release notes on Google Play.
